## How this article was researched

This guide is research-based. We did not sew these hems ourselves or test any machine for it. The method comes from a university extension publication (New Mexico State University, *Sewing Shortcuts*, C-104), machine settings from Singer and Brother support pages, needle guidance from Schmetz's fabric chart, and the definition from a reference work. Every figure is tied to a source in the page's citations. Where a source did not give a number, such as a stitch length for a standard woven hem, we left it out. See [how we review](/how-we-review/).
