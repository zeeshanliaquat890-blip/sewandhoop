## What this means for choosing a machine

You do not need an expensive machine to hem well. A straight stitch and a zigzag are enough for the methods above. Some machines add a blind hem stitch, and extension guidance lists the blind hem foot and rolled hem foot as feet that can cut down on sewing time <Cite id="C0204" />. A free arm (the narrow sewing bed that slides off) makes hemming sleeves and trouser legs easier.
