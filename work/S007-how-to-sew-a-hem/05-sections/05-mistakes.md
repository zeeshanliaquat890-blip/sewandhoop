## Common mistakes and how to fix them

**Wavy or stretched hem.** Usually the fabric was pulled while sewing, or the hem was not pressed first. Let the feed dogs (the small teeth under the foot) move the fabric, and steer lightly. On knits, a zigzag stitch and a damp pressing cloth help flatten ripples <Cite id="C0202" />.

**Skipped stitches or puckers.** Check the needle first. A dull needle or the wrong type is a frequent cause. For heavy fabric such as denim, Schmetz lists Jeans needles in 90/14 to 110/18 <Cite id="C0210" />.

**Skipping the test.** Different fabrics behave differently, so a scrap test saves a ruined garment <Cite id="C0207" />.

**Raw edge still fraying inside.** On medium to heavy fabrics, finish the raw edge with an overcast or zigzag stitch before folding. Singer's blind hem page makes this point: turn the edge under on fine fabrics, or overcast it on medium to heavyweight ones <Cite id="C0206" />.

**Uneven fold depth.** Use a ruler or seam gauge and press each fold before stitching, not after.
