## Where to buy and availability

Brother, Singer and Janome machines are sold on the makers' US sites, through authorized dealers and at large retailers. The product boxes above link to Amazon.com listings so you can check current availability. Stock and bundles change, and some retailers sell exclusive variants, such as the SE725, with different accessories. Match the exact model name before you order. Janome machines are mostly sold through dealers, so expect a price quote or a retailer page rather than a manufacturer checkout.
