## The ten picks

<ProductBox id="brother-se700">
**Best overall: Brother SE700.** A good fit if you want one machine for hemming, garment sewing and monogramming, and you are happy starting with small designs.

Brother lists a 4 x 4 inch maximum embroidery area <Cite id="C0032" />, which suits monograms, pocket designs and children's clothes. It includes 135 built-in embroidery designs <Cite id="C0033" /> and 103 built-in sewing stitches <Cite id="C0034" />, plus 10 buttonhole styles <Cite id="C0035" />. An automatic thread cutter <Cite id="C0036" /> saves you snipping thread tails between colors. Sewing speed tops out at 710 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0037" />, a maximum rather than a typical pace.

Brother lists a 1/2/25-year limited warranty <Cite id="C0038" />, which means different parts are covered for different lengths of time.

The limit is hoop size: 4 x 4 inches <Cite id="C0032" /> rules out jacket backs and large quilt blocks. Choose the SE2000 or an embroidery-only 5 x 7 inch machine if you already know you want bigger designs.

<BuySkip buy="You want sewing and embroidery in one machine and mostly stitch small designs." skip="You will only embroider, in which case the PE545 costs less, or you want larger designs." />
</ProductBox>

<ProductBox id="brother-pe545">
**Best budget embroidery machine: Brother PE545.** This is the low-cost way to start machine embroidery, and it sits in the lowest price band here ($400–$500). It does not sew regular seams.

It has the same 4 x 4 inch maximum embroidery area <Cite id="C0054" /> as the SE700, with 135 built-in designs <Cite id="C0055" /> and 10 built-in lettering fonts <Cite id="C0056" />. Brother lists an advanced needle threading system <Cite id="C0058" /> and an automatic thread cutter <Cite id="C0059" />. It is wireless-LAN enabled <Cite id="C0060" /> for sending files from a computer, and the warranty is 1/2/25-year limited <Cite id="C0061" />.

Embroidery speed tops out at 400 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0057" />, the slowest here, so expect longer stitch-outs. That matters little for monograms and a lot for production work.

<BuySkip buy="You want the least expensive way into embroidery and already own a sewing machine." skip="You also want to sew with it; the SE700 does both for a little more." />
</ProductBox>

<ProductBox id="singer-legacy-se300">
**Best for a larger hoop on a modest budget: Singer Legacy SE300.** It suits a sewist who wants a combo machine and a bigger stitching field than the SE700 without moving into the $1,000-plus bands.

Singer includes two hoops, a large 10.25 x 6 inch and a small 4 x 4 inch <Cite id="C0107" />. The wide hoop is good for borders and long monograms. You also get 250 built-in sewing stitches <Cite id="C0105" /> and 13 buttonhole styles sewn in one step <Cite id="C0108" />. There are 200 embroidery designs, though some arrive on an included USB stick rather than being built in <Cite id="C0106" />.

Check the software. Singer says the complementary design software works with Windows only <Cite id="C0112" />, so Mac users should plan another route for creating files. Singer lists up to 25 years of limited warranty coverage, with some restrictions <Cite id="C0113" />.

<BuySkip buy="You want a wide hoop and a full sewing machine at a mid-range band." skip="You use a Mac and want the maker's own design software, or you want 5 x 7 inch hoops from a Brother." />
</ProductBox>

<ProductBox id="singer-se9180">
**Best touch-screen combo: Singer SE9180.** A fit for someone who likes seeing designs on a big screen and wants sewing space for garments and quilts.

Singer lists a 7 inch color touch screen <Cite id="C0098" /> and 250 stitches <Cite id="C0093" /> alongside 150 embroidery designs <Cite id="C0094" /> and 10 fonts <Cite id="C0095" />. There are 6.5 inches between the needle and the tower <Cite id="C0101" />, which gives you room to maneuver a quilt, and a metal interior frame <Cite id="C0100" />. An automatic needle threader <Cite id="C0099" /> is included. Maximum speed is 800 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0097" />.

The included hoop is the catch: Singer lists one hoop for designs up to 170 x 100 millimeters, about 6.7 x 3.9 inches <Cite id="C0096" />, so the stitching field is small. It weighs 15.5 pounds <Cite id="C0102" />. Warranty is up to 25 years limited, with some restrictions <Cite id="C0103" />.

<BuySkip buy="You want a roomy sewing arm, a large screen and mostly small to medium embroidery." skip="You want a 5 x 7 inch field or larger; look at the SE2000 or PE900." />
</ProductBox>
