## How we chose these machines

We began with models that keep appearing in embroidery roundups and retailer lists, then checked each against the maker's current US product page. A machine needed a verified type, embroidery area (or stitch count for combos) and US warranty to be included. Several popular models were dropped: the Brother SE600, SE1900, PE800 and PE535 show as out of stock on Brother's site, and we could not confirm warranty terms for Janome's Memory Craft models from the maker's pages.

This is a research-based guide. We have not stitched on these machines. We also could not gather enough consistent owner reports to describe how any of them hold up over years, so you will not find claims about long-term reliability. Our full approach is on [how we review](/how-we-review/).

Order follows how well each machine's checked features fit a home embroiderer, not what pays us. The site earns a commission on Amazon.com purchases made through links here.
