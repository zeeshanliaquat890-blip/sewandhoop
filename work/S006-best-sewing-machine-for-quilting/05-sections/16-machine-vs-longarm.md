## Home machine or longarm?

A home sewing machine pieces a quilt top and can quilt the layers by pushing a rolled quilt under the arm. A longarm is a different machine: a long frame that holds the whole quilt while the sewing head moves over it. Longarms suit people who quilt many large quilts or sell their work, and they cost several times as much as any machine here and need a dedicated room.

For most home quilters, a good domestic machine with a large workspace is plenty. Many people piece at home and send finished tops to a longarm service. That is a good fit if you love piecing but not wrestling a king-size quilt. Our [quilting machines hub](/quilting-machines/) gathers the pieces on both options, and the longarm guides will be linked here when they are published.
