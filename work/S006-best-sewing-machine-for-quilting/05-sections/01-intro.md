The best sewing machine for quilting is the one that gives you enough room to the right of the needle, feeds several layers evenly and lets you drop the feed dogs (the small toothed bars under the fabric) when you want to quilt freely. For most home quilters who also sew clothes and home décor, the Brother Quilt Club BQ2500 is the strongest all-round pick here, and the Brother CS7000X is the budget pick.
