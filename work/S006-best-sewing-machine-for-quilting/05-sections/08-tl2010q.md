**Best straight-stitch workhorse: Juki TL-2010Q.** A good fit if your quilting is piecing, straight-line quilting and free-motion work, and you would rather have one stitch done well than 700 you will not use.

Juki says this machine sews straight stitch only <Cite id="C0160" />. That is the trade: no zigzag, no buttonholes, no decorative stitches, which also means fewer settings between you and a finished seam. It lists a top speed of 1,500 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0161" /> and a speed control lever that sets the speed anywhere from 200 to 1,500 <Cite id="C0167" />, so you can slow it to a crawl for careful free-motion work and still run long seams quickly.

For space, Juki lists an auxiliary table of 23 x 13 inches <Cite id="C0164" /> and a work area up to 23 inches with it attached; the page gives no throat figure. The feed dogs drop with a switch for free-motion quilting, and the machine lists needle up/down, a knee lifter and LED lighting <Cite id="C0166" />. It also lists an automatic thread trimmer button and a push-lever needle threader <Cite id="C0165" />. The frame is aluminum die-cast <Cite id="C0168" />, and the machine weighs 25.4 lb <Cite id="C0163" />. The maximum stitch length is 0.2 inch (6 mm) <Cite id="C0162" />.

Juki America lists a limited warranty of 2 years on motors and electronics and 5 years on other parts <Cite id="C0169" />, for the original purchaser in the US and Canada. Authorized dealer listings put the TL-2010Q between $1,000 and $1,500 <Cite id="C0170" />, with dealer sale prices varying.

The limit is the one on the box: straight stitch only. If you want to sew a buttonhole or a stretch seam, you need a second machine.
