**Best compact computerized Juki: Juki HZL-F600.** A good fit if you want a Juki that sews garments and quilts, and you have limited space or need to carry the machine to classes.

Juki lists 225 stitch patterns <Cite id="C0188" /> and 16 electronic buttonholes <Cite id="C0189" />. At 21.6 lb <Cite id="C0191" /> it is the lightest of the Juki machines here, so it carries to a class more easily than the 33 lb NX7. Top speed is 900 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0190" />. For quilting, Juki says the feed dogs drop with a switch for free-motion work <Cite id="C0193" />, and it lists a walking foot, a wide table and a knee lift lever among the accessories <Cite id="C0194" />. It also lists an automatic needle threader and three ways to trim thread <Cite id="C0192" />.

Juki America lists a limited warranty of 2 years on electronics and 5 years on other parts <Cite id="C0195" />. We could not verify a price band: dealer listings we saw disagreed widely, so ask an authorized Juki dealer.

The limit is space, because the Juki page gives no throat figure for the HZL-F600, so we cannot say how it compares with the Baby Lock Jazz II's 12 inches <Cite id="C0151" /> or the Juki NX7's <Cite id="C0181" />. Ask a dealer to show you a rolled quilt under the arm before buying if bed quilts are your goal.
