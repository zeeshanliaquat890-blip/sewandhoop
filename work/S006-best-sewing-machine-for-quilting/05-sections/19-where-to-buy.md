## Where to buy and availability

You have two routes: an authorized dealer or a large online store. A dealer is the usual route for the Baby Lock and Juki machines. It gives you a person to ask, a chance to sit at the machine with a rolled quilt, and local service. A dealer may also offer classes or a maintenance plan. Prices at dealers can be higher or lower than online, and many dealers match or discount, so ask.

Online stores, including Amazon.com for the models that are sold there, are convenient and often competitive on price for the Brother and Singer machines, but you will normally deal with the maker's support for problems. Availability changes: during our research, Brother's page showed the BQ1350 as discontinued and Singer's page showed the 9960 as out of stock, so check that the exact model you want is current. Avoid look-alike model numbers and retailer-exclusive bundles, which are different machines with different specs.

A short checklist before you click buy: confirm the exact model, check what is in the box, confirm the warranty applies in the US, and find out who services it near you.
