## Gap list
- Throat/workspace is rarely explained in numbers; "throat" vs "workspace" vs extension-table width get mixed up.
- Straight-stitch-only (Juki TL) vs computerized all-rounders is rarely explained plainly for non-technical buyers.
- Dealer-priced machines (Brother Quilt Club, Baby Lock, Juki) are listed with stale or sale prices.
- Warranty differences and where to get service (dealer vs online) are almost never covered.
- Popular models now discontinued/out of stock (Brother BQ1350, Singer 9960, Pfaff Quilt Ambition 630) are still recommended.
- Machine-vs-longarm decision is missing in most roundups.
