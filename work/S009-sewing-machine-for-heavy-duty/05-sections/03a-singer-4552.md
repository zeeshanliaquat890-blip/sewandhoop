**Best overall: Singer Heavy Duty 4552.** A good fit if you sew denim, canvas, vinyl and bag fabric regularly and want the machine that arrives best equipped for it, starting with the walking foot in the box <Cite id="C0232" />.

Singer includes an even feed (walking) foot and a non-stick foot <Cite id="C0232" />. A walking foot grips the top layer and moves it along with the bottom, which helps thick, slippery or layered fabric stay together. The 4552 lists adjustable presser foot pressure and drop feed <Cite id="C0233" />, a full metal interior frame and a stainless steel bedplate <Cite id="C0231" />, and speed control with an integrated needle threader <Cite id="C0230" />. Speed control lets you cap how fast the needle runs, which matters on thick seams.

It lists 32 built-in stitches <Cite id="C0227" />, a one-step buttonhole <Cite id="C0229" /> and a top speed of 1000 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0228" />, a maximum rather than a pace for thick fabric. Singer lists more than 6 inches to the right of the needle <Cite id="C0234" />. Its page lists a lifetime warranty on the metal frame, with some restrictions <Cite id="C0235" />; the page gives no terms for other parts here. It sits in the $200–$300 band <Cite id="C0236" />.

The limit is space. Six inches is workable for jeans and bags, but tight for large home-décor projects.
