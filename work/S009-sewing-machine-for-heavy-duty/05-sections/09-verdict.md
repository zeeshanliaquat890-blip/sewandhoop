## Verdict

<Verdict buyIf="You sew denim, canvas, vinyl or bags at home and want a walking foot, speed control and a metal frame in one machine: the Singer Heavy Duty 4552. On a tighter budget, the Brother ST371HD." skipIf="You sew thick leather or many layers all day, or only straight seams. Look at the Sailrite Ultrafeed LSZ, or the Juki TL-2000Qi and TL-2010Q.">
The Singer 4552 earns the top spot on checked features: a walking foot in the box, speed control and a full metal interior frame, in the $200–$300 band. The Brother ST371HD is the cheaper starting point, and the Sailrite and Juki machines suit specialist jobs.
</Verdict>
