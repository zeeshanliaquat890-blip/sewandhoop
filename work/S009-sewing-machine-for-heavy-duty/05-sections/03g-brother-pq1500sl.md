**Best for fast straight seams: Brother PQ1500SL.** A good fit if you sew long seams in thick or layered fabric and want speed and control more than decorative stitches.

Brother calls it a high-speed straight-stitch machine <Cite id="C0264" /> with a top speed of 1500 stitches per minute <Cite id="C0265" />. It has one built-in stitch <Cite id="C0266" />, so no zigzag and no buttonholes. In return it lists an automatic thread cutter and automatic needle threading <Cite id="C0267" />, seven feet including a walking foot and a knee lifter <Cite id="C0268" />, and an adjustable pin feed, four feed dog settings and adjustable presser foot pressure <Cite id="C0269" />. Work space is 5.7 by 8.6 inches <Cite id="C0270" />.

Brother lists a 2/6/25 year premium limited warranty <Cite id="C0271" /> and a $700–$800 band <Cite id="C0272" />; its page showed it out of stock when we checked.

The limit is plain: you will need a second machine for buttonholes and zigzag.
