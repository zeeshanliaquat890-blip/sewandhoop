# S009 research — sewing machine for heavy duty

## Page purpose and distinctness
A buying guide for the use case "heavy-duty sewing": what the term means, which fabrics and seams it covers, and ten current machines grouped by the job they do (denim, canvas, upholstery, bags, straight-seam speed). Distinct from S020 /best-heavy-duty-sewing-machine/ (a ranked "best" list; this page is the use-case explainer with picks by job), from S104 (Singer-only), and from the Singer 4423/4432/4411 review pages (one machine each). Published pages S004 (beginners) and S006 (quilting) feature 4423 and the Juki TL machines for other reasons.

## Gap list
- What "heavy duty" actually means (frame, motor, feed, needle) versus the label on a box.
- How thick is too thick: jeans hems and bulky seams; needle and presser-foot-pressure guidance.
- Home machine vs industrial/walking-foot machine: who needs which.
- Service and warranty reality for Singer/Brother/Janome vs Sailrite and Juki dealer machines.
- "Handheld heavy duty" machines: not covered (not machines for thick fabric; no verified source).
